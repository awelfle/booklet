A reading tracker for Are.na

Your reading list, kept on Are.na.

Booklet is a small app for your phone: the books you want to read, the ones you’re reading, and the ones you’ve finished. Search, tap, done. Your books live in your own Are.na channels — no new account, no server.

Get Booklet on GitHub Read the setup guide

Free and open source (MIT). Works with free Are.na accounts.

Everything a reading list needs, nothing it doesn’t

Made for adding a book in the bookshop, marking it started on the train, and rating it the night you finish.

◫

Three shelves

To Read, Reading and Read. Move a book along with one tap; Booklet notes when you started and finished.

⌕

Finds the book

Searches Open Library and Google Books together, so brand-new releases, typos and ISBNs all turn up.

❦

The right edition

Saves the U.S. print edition — hardcover first — with its cover, publisher, pages and ISBN.

★

Dates and ratings

Rate a book when you finish it, and fix any start or finish date after the fact.

▢

On your home screen

Add it to your phone and it opens like an app. Light and dark, sized for one thumb.

◎

Your data, your Are.na

Every book is a block in your own channels. Booklet has no server; your token stays on your device.

Everything about a book, one tap away

Tap any book for its edition details and your dates, with links out to read more or buy a copy.

  • Links to Bookshop, Open Library, Google Books and Amazon
  • “Finished it” asks for a rating; “Reading it again” starts a fresh read
  • Because it’s Are.na, every book is also a block you can connect to other channels

Set up in about ten minutes

Booklet is a web page you host yourself — free on Netlify, GitHub Pages or anything that serves files. The README walks through it.

  1. Deploy your copy

    Fork the repo and deploy it. There’s a one-click Netlify button, and no build step.

  2. Make an Are.na token

    Create a personal access token with write access in your Are.na settings.

  3. Create your shelves

    Open your copy, paste the token and tap “Create my shelves.” Then add it to your home screen.

Show what you’re reading

Booklet comes with a tiny widget that lists your Reading shelf on any website. No token needed — your shelves are viewable by anyone.

<div data-currently-reading="your-reading-channel"></div>

Questions

Do I need Are.na Premium?

No. Booklet doesn’t use any Premium-only features. Free accounts have lower API rate limits, which is plenty for books.

What does it cost?

Nothing. Booklet is free and open source, and static hosting is free on most hosts.

Where’s my data?

In your Are.na channels. Booklet stores only your token and shelf names, in your browser.

Is this made by Are.na?

No — Booklet is an independent project built on Are.na’s public API.
